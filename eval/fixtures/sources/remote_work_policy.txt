Northwind Robotics -- Remote Work Policy (Effective 2026-01-01)

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed at least 90 days of continuous employment and
whose role is designated "remote-eligible" by their manager may work
remotely up to 4 days per week. Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan
(PIP) are not eligible for remote work during the PIP period.

2. Core Hours
All remote employees must be reachable on company communication tools
between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone, Monday through
Friday. Outside of core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long
as they meet their weekly hour commitment.

3. Equipment Stipend
Northwind Robotics provides a one-time home office equipment stipend of
$750 to employees approved for remote work. The stipend can be used for a
desk, chair, monitor, or other ergonomic equipment, and must be claimed
through the Expense Portal within 60 days of remote-work approval.

4. Approval Process
Remote work requests must be submitted through the HR Portal and approved
by the employee's direct manager and by HR. Approval typically takes 5
business days. Remote work arrangements are reviewed every 12 months.

5. Security Requirements
Remote employees must use company-issued laptops with full-disk encryption
enabled and must connect to internal systems only through the corporate
VPN. Use of public Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited.
