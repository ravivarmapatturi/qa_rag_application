Northwind Robotics -- Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy (Effective 2026-01-01)

1. Meal Per Diem
Employees traveling for business receive a daily meal per diem of $65 for
domestic travel and $90 for international travel. The per diem covers
breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined and does not require itemized
receipts.

2. Receipt Requirements
Any single non-meal expense over $25 requires an itemized receipt uploaded
to the Expense Portal within 14 days of the expense date. Expenses without
a receipt above this threshold will not be reimbursed.

3. Approval Chain
Expense reports under $500 require approval from the employee's direct
manager only. Expense reports of $500 or more require approval from both
the direct manager and the finance department.

4. Airfare Class
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Flights of 6 hours or
longer qualify for premium economy. Business class requires VP-level
approval regardless of flight duration.

5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 10 business days via direct
deposit to the employee's payroll account.
